Supporting a Child with Autism

What this guide is

A free, practical companion for parents, caregivers, and friends. It focuses on understanding sensory needs, communication, routines, meltdowns, strengths, and daily strategies. Nothing replaces a professional, but this can help you think clearly and feel less alone.

Quick topics

  • Sensory processing and overload
  • Communication (verbal and non-verbal)
  • Routines and transitions
  • Meltdowns vs. tantrums
  • Strengths and special interests
  • School and social situations
  • Self-care for caregivers
