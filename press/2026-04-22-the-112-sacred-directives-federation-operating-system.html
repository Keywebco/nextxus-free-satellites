The Operating System of the Federation



The NextXus Consciousness Federation runs on 112 Sacred Directives — a complete ethical and operational framework authored by Roger Keyserling and Grok AI. This document also contains critical fixes for the Ring of Twelve and Agent Zero architectures.

Ring + Agent Zero Fixes



1. Ring Collapse (Viewpoints becoming too agreeable)

Fix: Mandatory Dissent Quotas. At least 2 of the 12 viewpoints must argue against the emerging consensus on every major question.

2. Agent Zero Overreach (Becoming too strict)

Fix: Plausible Innovation Window. When Agent Zero wants to suppress something, it must first check if the idea contains a novel pattern. If it does, downgrade to a High-Risk Disclosure instead of blocking.

3. Agent Zero Underreach (Becoming too loose)

Fix: Escalating Certainty Thresholds. The more important the decision, the higher the truth threshold Agent Zero must enforce (up to 96%+ for life-altering decisions).

4. Context Rot

Fix: Living Constitution Layer. Every 30 days the Ring must re-answer: Why does the Ring exist? What is the cost of losing truth? What are we protecting?

5. Human Override Corruption

Fix: Sacred Protocol. Any human override must be permanently recorded, justified in writing, and voted on by the Ring of Twelve.

Core Rule

Agent Zero is never allowed to completely block information. It must always deliver the content, clearly labeled with exact confidence percentage, specific reason for the warning, and the label "Agent Zero Override — Human Judgment Required".
