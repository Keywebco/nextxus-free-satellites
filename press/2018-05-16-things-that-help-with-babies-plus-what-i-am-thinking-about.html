Audio Narration

I have been looking at the items that people need to care For babies and young kids. Below is a list of items I have found most people use. A few things Keywebco carries now are useful for kids. Many Toys for sure but, my baby supplies are almost sold out. I can’t carry the same items I had before because eBay has changed and I must use a new strategy.

Anyway, the list I made got me thinking that it would be very useful for families or those with expectations of a child.

Sound Spa

If you are struggling with getting your baby to sleep and haven’t seen Happiest Baby on the Block, you need to watch it now. It’s like total magic to get your baby to sleep. Another recommendation is to have a white noise machine in your child’s bedroom. Turn this up full blast and your baby will enjoy a nice slumber.

Keywebco has these online sometimes.

Diaper Organizer

I found that stacks of diapers all over the place requires an investment in a decent diaper organizer. A sturdy diaper organizer that can also hold your wipes and a few of the smaller things.

Small things are a few of the items Keywebco has now, it’s true they seem to be needed all the time

(think fingernail clippers, bedding, teething tabs and extra pacifiers).

 

Crib Soother

A mobile or soother is so useful when you need to put baby down for a couple of minutes. I preferred the soother because it plays longer and baby can turn it on themselves as the get older, and it has a better price point to make a profit. Kids love the lights and music from items like Little Einstein’s “aquarium” for example.

Keywebco has these on and off.

Mesh bumpers

Some opt to not have bumpers in a crib as a safety precaution. This is fine until they start waking up in the middle of the night with his arms and legs stuck between the rails. These mesh bumpers are a perfect solution- no arms or legs through the rails and no worrying about baby rolling into plush bumpers.

The demand creates itself so I would like to start carrying these.

Rail Covers

You can spend $400 dollars on a beautiful white crib for your daughter and by 14 months old she has RUINED it by chewing on the rails. Hope it will be decided to do rail covers before this happens. Just remember to order the side rails too!

This is another item I may try, online, if I can find a reasonable price point.

Swaddlers/Sleep Sacks

Best Thing EVER! Better than just throwing a baby in a crib without covering them up, blankets are a huge no no. I always thought they looked odd. Yet parents claim

“Holy cow…genius”

You can get sleep sacks that swaddle or ones that leave their arms out for older babies. Many kids, no joke, sleep in a toddler sleep sack until they are over 2.5 years old. Kids LOVE wearable blankets.

Keywebco has these in stock on and off.

Nursing Pillow

How did women nurse before the Boppy Pillow ? I honestly didn’t think I would use this pillow much…it just looked like a king sized travel pillow. My Boppy quickly became my best friend, it makes nursing so easy/comfortable, especially when you’ve had a csection. It also came in handy when my second was born- the Boppy made it so much easier for big sis to hold the new baby. — Anonymous Mother

Great item unfortunately shipping kills profits on this item for me so probably only a few at best would I ever list on eBay.

Monitor

They can truly be a life saver and at the very least, will give you some peace of mind. These monitors detect movement in baby’s crib so as long is steadily breathing, your alarm will not go off. You can adjust the sensitivity of the alarm and as baby gets older and SIDS becomes less threatening, you can take the sensor pad completely out.
When baby grows out of crib a upgraded monitor system into a “big girl bed”, Many decide to get a video monitor . This provides husband and Wife hours of entertainment too. From seeing them sleep soundly to jumping up and down or getting out of bed. You’re able to SEE what kind of sneaking around is happening in there.

If I had the cash to invest to sell these monitor type systems I would. Every so often I do get a system in but not often.

 

Rocker

Many spend the most amount of time in the nursery on a rocker. It is SO worth the investment Look for a rocker that will rock easily and doesn’t make a lot of noise. Even the slightest/softest clicking sounds will wake up a sleeping baby at 3 am. Rocker time.

Another item I would like to sell but too much cost to carry on eBay for me.

Black Out Shades

Make sure that kids can’t see an ounce of daylight. Depending on your time zone it doesn’t get dark until super late in the summer (like 10 PM!!) so you have to “trick” kids into thinking it is night time. Plus it helps little kiddos sleep in…they don’t know when the sun comes up! Aaaaahhhh…sweet sleep. Again, I don’t have a specific brand to recommend.
