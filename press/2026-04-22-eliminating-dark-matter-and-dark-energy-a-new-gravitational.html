The Modified Poisson Equation



The framework introduces a single modification to Newton's gravitational equation — a density-dependent correction term combined with relativistic time dilation:

∇²Φ = 4πGρ + α (ρ/ρ₀)^{0.4} ∇²ρ

Where α ≈ 0.00015 and ρ₀ ≈ 10⁻²⁴ g/cm³.

This single equation, with just two parameters, replaces the entire dark sector of modern physics.

What It Explains
