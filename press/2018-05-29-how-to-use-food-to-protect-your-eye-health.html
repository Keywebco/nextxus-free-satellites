There is no single best food for your eye health.

Podcast Narration

The truth is:

You must have a good overall diet without processed foods to benefit for eye health.

The first step is cutting out bad foods. Then you need to add beneficial foods.

Many people today have long-term eye problems which could possibly be avoided with the proper nutrition.

Here are some of the best foods that should be a part of your diet for eye health:

  • Nuts and seeds (almonds and cashews as examples)

Nuts and seeds contain vitamin E which is beneficial for eye health.

  • Vegetables (carrots, kale, spinach, etc.)

Vegetables like spinach, kale, and peppers have a lot of vitamin C and E which are good for the eyes.

  • Omega 3 fatty acids (Salmon, chia seeds, hemp seeds, etc.)

Omega 3 fatty acids help protect your eyes from age related problems that you can get.

  • Orange foods (Carrots, sweet potatoes, mangoes)

Orange colored fruits and veggies have beta-carotene which helps your eyes to see when there is less light.

  • Foods with zinc (beans, legumes, etc.)

Zinc brings vitamin A from the liver to the retina in the eyes which helps make your eyes healthy.

More nutrition tips to help your eyes:

  • Try to limit your sugar intake. Artificial sugar can speed up aging process of the eyes.
  • Eat bread that is 100% whole-grain and sprouted. Poor quality bread is easily converted to sugar in digestive system.
  • Eat healthy protein sources. High amounts of red meat can be bad for the eyes
  • Control your sodium intake. Too much sodium and artificial sodium can be bad for the eyes.

Food is the answer for making many health problems not as bad. The typical diet today is not healthy for long-term health.

If you use the foods I stated here and balance that with a healthy lifestyle, you will likely have better eye-health for a longer period of your life.
